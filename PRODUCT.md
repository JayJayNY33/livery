# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Lucide icons (specified by the user). Deploy target: GitHub + Vercel.

## Users

- Patients on Long Island who need rides to medical appointments, many of them older, on dialysis, or in ongoing treatment, often reading on a phone.
- Family members and caregivers arranging rides for a patient.
- Healthcare coordinators at medical offices, rehabilitation centers, and nursing homes.

Their job: find out whether Fast Track can take them, how to get Fast Track assigned through MAS, and reach the office by phone.

## Product Purpose

Fast Track Medical & Livery Transportation provides non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) from a Long Island, NY base. The website exists to earn trust and make it effortless to call the office or request Fast Track as a preferred provider through Medical Answering Services (MAS). Success is a phone call.

## Positioning

A small, personal NEMT provider: the same faces, attention to recurring riders, and effort to arrange earlier pickups after long treatments such as dialysis. "At Fast Track, you are more than just another ride."

## Operating Context

- Medicaid rides are arranged through MAS (1-844-666-6270); riders may ask that Fast Track be assigned. Assignment is subject to MAS requirements, authorization, and availability.
- Regular rides: contact MAS at least three days ahead. Cancellations: at least four hours' notice requested.
- Standing orders for recurring appointments depend on eligibility, approval, and scheduling; no guaranteed driver or provider.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fleet: standard sedans, including Hyundai Sonatas and Elantras. NOT wheelchair accessible; passengers cannot remain seated in a wheelchair. Folding wheelchairs/walkers may be carried if they fit securely in the trunk, confirmed in advance.
- Urgent/last-minute rides cannot be guaranteed. Not an ambulance service; emergencies call 911.
- Medical transportation only on the site; no non-medical livery claims (user decision).
- Contact form has no backend yet; it must not pretend to send.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Fast Track Medical & Livery Transportation ("Fast Track Car Service" in FAQ copy).
- Logo: supplied charcoal/red/white sign artwork (car silhouette, medical cross, FASTTRACK wordmark). Use a cropped, background-removed version on navy; red appears only inside the logo.
- Palette pinned by the user: deep navy primary, medium blue for interaction, white and light blue-gray grounds.
- Voice: professional, dependable, compassionate, friendly. Tagline: "Your Health. Your Appointments. Our Commitment to Getting You There."

## Product Principles

1. The phone number is always one tap away.
2. Never overstate coverage: eligibility, authorization, and availability qualifiers travel with every Medicaid statement.
3. Say the limits plainly (sedans only, not wheelchair accessible, not an ambulance).
4. Dignity over speed: warmth and respect in every line.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Older riders and caregivers: large readable type, high contrast, generous tap targets, full keyboard support, WCAG 2.2 AA.
